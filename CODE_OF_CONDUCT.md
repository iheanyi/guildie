# Code of Conduct

Guildie follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Short version: be kind, assume good intent, keep feedback about the work, and
no harassment. That goes for issues, pull requests, reviews and anywhere else
we talk about Guildie.

Report problems to the maintainer (@natedunn) privately. Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads or block people
who don't follow this.
